# MVP scope

This scope reflects the interview. It has not yet been estimated or divided into delivery phases.

## Patient management

- Registration with required name and phone number.
- Optional additional information, completable by doctors.
- Shared phone numbers with duplicate warning and family-member note.
- Patient medical history, allergies, and conditions.
- Doctor access based on arrival and subsequent care.
- Standalone historical patient debt assigned to a doctor.

## Clinical care

- Interactive adult tooth chart.
- Multiple treatment plans per patient.
- Catalog procedures with default prices and custom procedure entry.
- Single-tooth, multiple-tooth, and whole-mouth procedures.
- Acceptance tracked per procedure.
- Standalone procedures outside treatment plans.
- Planned, in-progress, done, and stopped procedure states.
- Resumption of stopped procedures using the same record.
- One treating doctor per procedure.
- Multiple visits per procedure.
- Visit notes locked after the visit, with dated amendments.
- X-ray, photo, and document attachments linked to the patient, procedure, or visit.
- Staff X-ray upload with a single upload preview, then filename and confirmation only.
- Prescriptions using a shared medicine list or free text.
- Optional saving of newly entered medicines to the shared list.
- Clinic-branded prescriptions with doctor name and phone in the footer.

## Appointments

- Scheduling by doctor.
- Individual working hours, breaks, and time off.
- Owner and doctor management of doctor availability.
- Appointment duration chosen per booking.
- Overlapping appointments blocked.
- Reserved times grayed out with a warning.
- Actual visit timing separate from booked timing.
- Start/finish buttons and manual time corrections.
- Walk-ins booked into available slots only.
- Existing appointments flagged when availability changes affect them.
- Manual WhatsApp reminder shortcut with a prepared message.

## Payments and documents

- Flexible installments without scheduled due dates.
- Current procedure suggested for payment allocation.
- Cash and manually verified mobile money payments.
- Owner-managed mobile money app list.
- Doctor-associated patient credit.
- Excess payment allocation and refunds.
- Payment correction approval workflows.
- Consultation fees configurable clinic-wide or per doctor.
- Returning consultation fees applied or waived after staff consults the doctor.
- Price and discount changes with history and required reasons after treatment starts or payments exist.
- Receipt after each payment.
- Printed treatment plans.
- Optional invoices containing only completed, fully paid procedures.
- French printed documents.

## Finances and compensation

- Collections by date and payment method.
- Patient debt reports.
- Revenue reporting by doctor and procedure; exact revenue definition remains unresolved.
- Clinic expenses, including lab and material costs.
- Doctor salary, percentage, or both.
- Fixed monthly staff wages.
- Gross or after-cost percentage basis per doctor.
- One percentage rate or procedure-type rates.
- Cost carry-forward against later payments for the same procedure.
- Earnings, actual payouts, and remaining compensation owed.
- Doctor access to their own compensation.
- Refund-related earnings reversals.
- Opening debt with a historical percentage and unpaid doctor compensation cap.

## Platform

- Responsive web app.
- Arabic, French, and English interface.
- Single clinic-wide currency label.
- Read-only offline access to previously loaded information.

## Future features

- Multiple clinics and organizations.
- Child teeth and mixed dentition.
- Multiple doctors treating the same procedure.
- Automatic WhatsApp reminders — could-have.
- Offline editing and synchronization.
- Additional printed-document languages.
- Dedicated tablet and desktop applications.
- Supplier balances and supplier payment tracking.
- Advanced business intelligence beyond the agreed reports.

## Unresolved details

Unspecified rules are not assumed. These include account administration, detailed document fields, file limits, numbering, payroll timing, rounding, report formulas, and certain credit/cost interactions.
